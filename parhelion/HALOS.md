# Halo & droplet catalogue (from atoptics.org.uk)

Tracking list for Parhelion, compiled from Les Cowley's Atmospheric Optics
(https://www.atoptics.org.uk/halo/contents.htm and the droplet/rainbow pages).
Face numbers: basal 1–2, prism 3–8, upper pyramid 13–18, lower pyramid 23–28.

Status: **✓** renders today · **habit** needs a new crystal shape ·
**orient** needs a new orientation · **mode** needs a new geometry/source mode ·
**wave** needs wave optics (not faked) · **unexplained** per the site.

## Next principled additions (priority)

1. Per-orientation tilt distributions (Gaussian σ and uniform-with-tails).
2. Irregular hexagons (independent side lengths): Kern arc, better Lowitz.
3. Restricted Lowitz rotation range; Lowitz columns.
4. Material table (ice, water, seawater, CO₂, NH₃, glass) with dispersion.
5. Finite-distance lamp source in a crystal volume: light pillars, lamp halos.
6. Wave optics for drops (Airy/Mie phase functions): supernumeraries, fogbow, corona, glory.
